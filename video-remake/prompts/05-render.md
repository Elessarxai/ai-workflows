# Step 5: Render and ship

One full render, then the checks that matter before posting.

## Prompt

```text
Render the full video. Then give me a contact sheet (one frame every 3 seconds) so I can check the pacing, the file size, and a 560px GIF made with palettegen for my GitHub README.
```

## Notes

Don't rewatch it ten times: by the third time your eye stops seeing problems. Ask Claude for the 3 weakest moments with timestamps instead (a trick from [Kylo](https://youtu.be/yVCc0NbZVbQ)), fix those, and watch it once on your phone before posting. That is how most people will see it.

Before you post on X: frame 0 is your thumbnail, so it has to be a finished picture (no black screen, no fade in, no glitch). Keep it readable muted. Avoid white flashes and warm, skin-toned blurred gradients: a text-only motion graphic of mine got my account locked for 12 hours by X's automatic filter. Post the MP4, the GIF is for the README.
