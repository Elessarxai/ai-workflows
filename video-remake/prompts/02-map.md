# Step 2: Map their structure to your content

This is the step that makes the video yours.

## Prompt

```text
Here's what my video is about: [paste your post, article or a few lines on the topic].

Using BREAKDOWN.md, map every element of the reference to my content in a table:
reference element | what it means there | my version | the data it shows

Keep the structure and the rhythm. Change every word, number and name. Flag anything where my content has no honest equivalent. Any real name, ticker or number must be true and I'll verify it; anything made up gets labelled as demo.

Also list what we must not take from the reference: their logo, characters, handle and anything with their name on it.

Suggest 1-2 layout changes so the result is clearly mine, not a copy.

Then write a beat sheet: every key moment with its second and the one thing a viewer must be able to read right then. Frame 0 is a beat too, because on X it's the thumbnail.

Save it all as MAPPING.md.
```

## Notes

Verify every real name and number in MAPPING.md before step 3. Trackers and aggregators are wrong more often than you think.

The beat sheet is what you check the stills against in step 4. Without it, "looks fine" is the only test you have.
