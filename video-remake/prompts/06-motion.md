# Extra: Make the motion look expensive

Almost always the timing: things moving at constant speed, all at once, with nothing settling. Put [motion.js](../motion.js) in your folder first.

## Prompt

```text
Use motion.js for every animation in index.html, with no numbers typed inline. Swap any constant-speed move for one of its curves, give the one hero element in each beat a spring at 0.8, and show me stills before and after.
```

## Notes

Turn feelings into numbers before you send them:

- "It looks cheap" → ease-out on everything that enters, one spring on the hero.
- "It's too busy" → one hero move at a time, the rest wait for it to land.
- "I can't read it" → fewer words, bigger type, a longer hold.
- "It looks like AI" → name the defaults it used (glow, purple gradients, everything fading up from below) and ban each one by name.

Phone rules: text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast on every frame, and nothing flashes more than 3 times a second (WCAG).
