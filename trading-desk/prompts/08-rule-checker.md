# Job 8: Make your own rules impossible to ignore

**When:** After you exit (and before the next entry)  
**You need:** Your trade in one line. Rules come from your DESK PROFILE.

## Prompt

```text
Check this trade against my rules. Use the rules in my DESK PROFILE (project instructions). If there's no profile, ask me for my rules once. I'll describe the trade in one line; ask only for what you need to check it.

Go rule by rule:
rule | pass or fail | why

Then:
1. Count the rules it breaks.
2. For each broken rule, what would have to change for it to pass (smaller size, different stop, wait for a condition).
3. Flag any sign I'm trading emotionally: revenge after a loss, FOMO after a pump, sizing up after a win.

Don't soften anything and don't argue me into the trade. If a rule is too vague to check, say so and suggest a clearer version.
```

## Notes

It's the most boring job here and probably the one that saves the most money.
