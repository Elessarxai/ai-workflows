# Job 5: A morning brief that only covers your bags

**When:** While you hold  
**You need:** Web search on. Positions come from your DESK PROFILE.

## Prompt

```text
Run my morning brief. Use the positions in my DESK PROFILE (project instructions). If there's no profile, ask me what I hold in one short question.

For each position, search the web for news from the last 24 hours: unlocks or vesting, exploits, listings or delistings, governance votes, lawsuits or regulator actions, team or treasury wallet moves, major product news.

Output:
1. A table: position | what happened | source link | why it matters to me (one line) | urgency (act today, watch, ignore).
2. Anything scheduled in the next 7 days that could move these positions.

Only include items with a link I can open, primary sources first. No general market news. If nothing happened for a position, write "nothing new".
```

## Notes

The last sentence keeps it honest. Without it you get filler every morning. If your setup supports scheduled tasks, run it every day before you wake up, and the brief is waiting with your coffee.
