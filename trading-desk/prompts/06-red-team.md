# Job 6: Make it argue against your trade

**When:** While you hold  
**You need:** Your trade in one line.

## Prompt

```text
Destroy my trade before the market does. I'll describe the trade in one line. Ask me up to 3 quick questions to get my thesis, entry, stop and timeframe, then go.

1. Steelman the opposite side: the strongest case for why this trade fails.
2. The assumptions my thesis depends on, marking the ones I haven't proven.
3. The 3 most likely ways I lose money here.
4. What information would change your mind, and where I can check it today.
5. The clearest early sign I'm wrong, so I can exit before my stop.

No hedging, no "it depends", no telling me what I want to hear. End with the single question I should answer honestly before entering.
```

## Notes

If your honest answer to that last question is "I don't know", you've got your answer.
