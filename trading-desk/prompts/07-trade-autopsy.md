# Job 7: Do an autopsy on your own trades

**When:** After you exit  
**You need:** Your trade history export (CSV or spreadsheet) from any exchange or journal.

## Prompt

```text
Do an autopsy on my trades. I'll attach my trade history export (CSV or spreadsheet) from any exchange or journal. Figure out the columns yourself and tell me which ones you found and which are missing.

Compare my losing trades with my winners:
1. Time of day and day of week.
2. Position size compared to my average.
3. Holding time.
4. What I did right after a loss (next trade size, time until the next trade).
5. Which assets or setups lose me the most.

Output the 3 habits costing me the most, each with the numbers that prove it (trade count, total lost), and one rule I could adopt tomorrow for each.

Show the counts behind every pattern. If a pattern rests on fewer than 10 trades, say it might be noise. Be blunt.
```

## Notes

Most people find one ugly habit in the first answer. Sizing up right after a loss is the classic.
